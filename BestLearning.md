## 📋 CORE PRINCIPLES (Foundation)

### 1. Generate as many INSIGHTS as possible
- Insights = random thoughts, purposes, examples
- Gained from EXPERIENCE in different ways
- Become an "insight machine"

### 2. Treat learning like PROBLEM SOLVING
- Leverage brain's insane problem-solving capacity
- Brain excels at both thinking and remembering

### 3. CARE about what you're doing
- Makes everything easier
- Essential for all other steps

### 4. Understand SMALL PIECES at a time
- Don't overwhelm yourself
- Break down → tackle one by one

### 5. FORGET MEMORIZATION
- Memorization = short term
- This method = FOREVER
- Rewiring brain to accept as truth

## STEP 1: UNDERSTAND THE BIG PICTURE

### Start with EVERYTHING ABSTRACTED OUT

```
CONCEPT = [Simple English Explanation]
          ↓
    ┌──────────────┐
    │  MAGIC BOX   │  ← Don't know what's inside yet
    │  BLACK BOX   │  ← Just know it works somehow
    └──────────────┘
```

**Key Points:**
- Know what purpose it serves
- NOT necessarily details or how it works
- As LITTLE information as possible
- Puts everything into context
- Makes it important to you

---

## STEP 2: LEARN SPECIFIC DETAILS

### For EACH detail, do these 3 sub-steps:

---

### 2A. UNDERSTAND THE DETAIL IN CONTEXT

**"Think OUTSIDE THE BOX"**

```
[Step A] → [Step B] → [MAGIC BOX] → [Step D] → [Result]
   ↑          ↑                         ↑          ↑
   │          │                         │          │
   └──────────┴─────────────────────────┴──────────┘
        Understand these parts while ignoring the box
```

**What to do:**
- Assume magic box works
- Everything OUTSIDE should make sense independently
- Understand WHY each step
- Understand WHAT each step accomplishes
- How pieces fit together
- How to use conclusion of one part for next part

**Process:**
- Pull ONE piece out of abstraction at a time
- (Sometimes need multiple pieces - do minimally)
- Break down until pieces can't be broken further

---

### 2B. UNDERSTAND THE PIECE BY ITSELF

---

#### 🔥 CRITICAL: "GIVING A DAMN" (Ancient 1400s Tech)

**Convince your brain to CARE**

##### Ways to care:
1. **Be PASSIONATE about subject**
2. **Be MOTIVATED by end result** (job, prestige)
3. **Take it PERSONALLY / Hit to your EGO**
   - "This speck of info is making a fool of you!"
   - "Nothing is beyond your reach"
   - "You CAN learn this - defend yourself!"

##### Benefits of caring:
- ✓ Remember for longer
- ✓ Brain generates more insights
- ✓ Easier time learning

---

#### Understand THE PROBLEM (the point of this piece)

**Questions to answer:**
- What does this piece accomplish?
- What does it describe?
- What problem does it solve?

**🔥 SEAR THE PROBLEM INTO YOUR HEAD**
→ Solution will be much more natural

---

#### 💡 TRY TO INVENT THE PIECE YOURSELF

**The Process:**
- Solve the problem yourself BEFORE seeing solution
- For rules: look at examples, reverse engineer
- Let brain explore possible avenues
- Deeply internalize the problem

##### Engage BOTH brains:

**CONSCIOUS brain:**
- Active thinking

**UNCONSCIOUS brain:**
- Develops intuition
- Example: Perfect comeback 10 hours later
- Brain silently processes when you care

##### When stuck on hard problem:
- → Brain convinced it's important
- → Remember it for long time
- → Force brain to care = it puts in work for you

**🚨 DON'T FAKE TRY - Make HONEST EFFORT**

> "People invented this. You're people. You can too."

---

##### Decision Tree:

```
Try to invent
     │
     ├─→ Successfully invented it? → Move to next step
     │
     └─→ Can't solve yet?
            │
            ▼
         Try until:
         • Completely tired
         • No more benefit from trying
            │
            ▼
         LOOK AT SOLUTION or get HINTS
```

---

#### PROCESS THE SOLUTION

**You created a GAP in your brain - solution fills it**

##### How to process:
- Think ACTIVELY about how it solves problem
- Use BOTH conscious & unconscious brain
- Use same abstraction strategies (big picture first)
- How it fits into big picture
- What insights it offers
- How someone thought of this solution
- What inspired this solution

##### Result: REWIRED BRAIN
- Mentally capable of processing problem & solution
- Primal brain convinced it's survival-important
- Remember MUCH longer

---

### 2C. REINFORCE IT (Keep it in your brain)

**Do for EACH piece AND for WHOLE concept**

---

## STEP 3: REINFORCEMENT PROCESS (4 Sub-Steps)

### Goal: Look at concept from different angles & Get as many insights as possible

---

### 3A. TRY TO INVENT THE CONCEPT/PIECE AGAIN

- If just did it → move to next step
- If not → do the invention process again
- Over time: burn mental association in head
- **Goal:** When you see problem → solution is first thought

**🏆 If you can invent it → YOU WIN**
- Intuition = the concept
- Embedded into thinking
- Brain rewired to accept association

---

### 3B. PRACTICE WITH IT (Most Important Step)

**What to do:**
- Find other RELEVANT PROBLEMS
- Apply concept wherever you can
- EXPAND on concept (not just mechanical application)
- Think: WHY concept fits the problems
- Think: WHY it might NOT fit
- Apply to HARD problems
- Force yourself to think
- Allow brain to get USED to applying concept
- Embed concept into your reasoning

**Benefits:**
- ✓ Gain insights you couldn't get from theory alone
- ✓ Forces viewing from different angles
- ✓ Context of problems makes more sense

---

### 3C. EXPLAIN THE CONCEPT

> "You don't understand until you can explain to someone who doesn't understand"

**Requirements:**
- Explain in YOUR OWN WORDS
  - Proves understanding is yours, not someone else's
- Start from SCRATCH
- Work through all details

**Testing understanding:**
- If YOU get confused → don't understand well enough
- If THEY get confused & you can't explain → don't understand

**Benefits:**
- ✓ Proves your understanding
- ✓ Forces you to find simpler representation
- ✓ Simplifies your whole understanding
- ✓ Reveals gaps to improve on

---

### 3D. EXPLORE (Ask Yourself Dumb Questions - Terrence Tao)

**Mentally explore RANDOM VARIATIONS:**

#### Change and Question:
- Change one part → does it still work? Why/why not?
- Change problem → does concept still apply?
- Does other solution apply to same scenario?
- Does past knowledge apply to this concept?
- Question intuitive assumptions - think harder

#### Test and Break:
- Try to BREAK the concept
- Come up with weird examples / counter-examples
- Prove it still works
- Look from different angles
- Try random stuff - see if it leads anywhere

#### Collaborate:
- Talk to OTHERS - explore with them
  - Ask about their experience
  - Ask about their insights

#### Deep Questioning:
- Ask WHY for every step and setup
- Challenge every part of concept
- Engage as much as possible
- Think about what's intuitive to you
- Apply as much as possible

**🎯 Goal:** As many INSIGHTS as possible for deeper understanding

---

## STEP 4: REPEAT REINFORCEMENT PROCESS OFTEN

### When to repeat:
- Feel like you're starting to forget
- Some pieces becoming unclear

### What to do:
- Try to invent from scratch AGAIN AND AGAIN
- Do all other reinforcement steps
- "Trap concept in mind - keep it there"

### Over time:

```
Reinforce more → Need to do less frequently
      ↓
Eventually won't have to do it at all
      ↓
PERMANENTLY SEARED INTO BRAIN
```

### Even after mastery:
- Play with concept occasionally
- Do practice to keep fresh
