## 💡 **Pro Tips for Success**

### **General Advice:**
1. **Consistency > Intensity** - Study daily, even if just 30 minutes
2. **Document Everything** - Use Notion, Obsidian, or OneNote
3. **Learn in Public** - Share your journey on Twitter/LinkedIn
4. **Join Communities** - Don't learn in isolation
5. **Practice Hands-On** - Theory alone won't cut it

### **Bug Bounty Specific:**
1. **Start Small** - Begin with "Good for Beginners" programs
2. **Read Disclosed Reports** - Learn from successful submissions
3. **Focus on One Vulnerability** - Master XSS before moving to SQLi
4. **Automate Recon** - Build a toolkit for efficiency
5. **Be Patient** - First valid bug may take weeks/months
6. **Quality Over Quantity** - One good report > 10 mediocre ones
7. **Build Relationships** - Network with other researchers

### **Learning Techniques:**
- 🧠 Use the Feynman Technique (teach what you learn)
- 🧠 Implement spaced repetition for retention
- 🧠 Create mind maps for complex topics
- 🧠 Build a lab environment for safe testing
- 🧠 Watch vulnerability writeups and replicate them

Remember: **Cybersecurity is a marathon, not a sprint.** This 90-day plan is just the beginning of your journey. The field constantly evolves, so commit to lifelong learning.

- ⚠️ Only test systems you have explicit permission to test
